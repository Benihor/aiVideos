How the script becomes a video

  1. Your text is split by subjects, clauses, and short phrases — not only by sentence length.
  2. Each shot gets a matching stock photo chosen for that subject.
  3. Optional captions show the words in a box while text to speech reads the full script.
  4. Pan, zoom, boom moves, and fades keep the cut visually active.
  5. Download the MP4. Source credits stay available when stock media was used.

Short scripts still produce a full video: visual subjects are expanded into multiple shots with different images.
